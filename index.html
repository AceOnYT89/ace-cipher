Ace Cipher

A Caesar cipher where each shift comes from letter order: A=1, B=2, C=3 … Z=26.

How it works

Each letter of your key gives a shift equal to its place in the alphabet. With the key ACE the shifts are 1, 3, 5, then they repeat. The 1st letter moves 1 place, the 2nd moves 3, the 3rd moves 5, the 4th moves 1 again, and so on. Decoding moves letters back by the same amounts. Spaces, numbers and punctuation are left alone and don’t use up a shift. Case is kept.
